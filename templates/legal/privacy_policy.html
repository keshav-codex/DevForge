Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DevForge handles information and permissions when you use the application.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. About DevForge

DevForge is a learning and portfolio project created to demonstrate web development, authentication, email integration, reporting, and AI/API functionality. It is not intended to operate as a large commercial or enterprise service.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, DevForge may process your name, email address, Google profile information, profile photo, authentication information, and other information required to provide application features.

3. Google Sign-In

DevForge may use Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in with Google, information such as your name, email address, and profile image may be provided according to the permissions granted during authentication.

This information is used to create and maintain your DevForge account and provide authenticated features.

4. Gmail Access and Email Sending

Some features may request the gmail.send permission so DevForge can send emails through your authorized Gmail account.

Emails are sent using the Gmail account you authorize. You are responsible for the recipients, message content, attachments, and other information included in emails you choose to send.

DevForge does not request broader Gmail access than required for the implemented functionality.

5. AI and External APIs

Certain features may use artificial intelligence or external APIs for analysis, summaries, reports, or other functionality.

Information sent to these services depends on the feature being used and the data required to perform that operation. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Storage

Application information may be stored in the application's database and associated storage systems. The information stored depends on the features you use.

7. Cookies and Sessions

DevForge may use cookies and server-side sessions to maintain authentication, security, preferences, and application functionality.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application information and authentication credentials. However, no internet-based application can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Deletion and Access Revocation

You may request deletion of your DevForge account or associated information by contacting the project developer through the contact information provided on the application or portfolio website.

Where applicable, you may also revoke DevForge's Google access through your Google Account settings.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, please contact the DevForge project developer using the contact information provided on the application or portfolio website.
